About

Bio

Shaked Miller is a Berlin-based rapper, singer and producer writing in English and Hebrew. Live, it's Eminem meets Rage Against the Machine. She produced Yeled's "Gveret Taltalim" (Universal Music Israel), writes and performs in Waves Audio campaigns, and has opened for Ninet Tayeb and Oi Va Voi.

Long bio

Shaked Miller blends punchy rap with sultry R&B, drawing on Eminem, Justin Timberlake and Lauryn Hill. She raps and sings about Jewish identity, loving women and the freedom to be who you are without apologising – honest and unapologetic, because humanity is weird.

Her debut album Best of This World – songs about self-exploration and sexuality, released with Kamea (2020–2023) – introduced singles like "Hold On", "Get The Girl", "Clouds" and "Take It All Away" with Gal De Paz. Her new, unreleased material pairs raw lyrics with Y2K-flavoured production, covering self-reflection, social critique and long-term partnership.

Behind the desk she produced and arranged Yeled's "Gveret Taltalim" (1.3M+ Spotify streams, Apple Music Israel Top 100), writes and records as a songwriter and vocalist for Artlist Originals, and has fronted Waves Audio ads and social content since 2023. She executive-produced Noyo's "Not Here" and produces Shiran Levy. She co-wrote on Gal De Paz's album Very That and featured on Guy Moses' "Priorities".

On stage she has been featured vocalist and substitute lead MC with Lucille Crew (2022–2025), opened for Ninet Tayeb at Hangar 11 and for Oi Va Voi at EartH – her first official London show – headlined Kuli Alma, played the Israel Music Showcase Festival and held a monthly residency at The George Hotel's Jazz Hall. In October 2026 she headlined Art Club TLV with her band. She performs as a trio (vocals + Ableton, drums, guitar) or an acoustic duo.
